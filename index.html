01 — ABOUT THE PROJECT

Transforming Traffic Management with AI

Traditional traffic signals operate using fixed timing, which often leads to unnecessary waiting and congestion. This project introduces an intelligent, adaptive approach that uses real-time traffic data to make better decisions.

The Problem

Urban roads experience heavy congestion because traditional traffic signals cannot adapt to changing traffic conditions. Fixed signal timings may keep vehicles waiting even when one lane has significantly higher traffic than others.

Our Solution

The AI-Powered Smart Traffic Management System analyzes live traffic footage using YOLOv7-based vehicle detection. It estimates traffic density and dynamically adjusts signal timings to improve traffic flow and reduce unnecessary waiting time.
